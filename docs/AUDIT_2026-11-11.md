# Game audit, 2026-11-11

Scope: logic, game design, controls (Windows and Android touch), visuals and art.
Build: `main` at v0.2.1-alpha plus M37.1. No code or data was changed.

## How this was checked

- **Played** the web build (v0.2.1-alpha) in a browser: title, "Who are you?", the gate, a talk, the walk to the Floodplains.
- **Rendered** 24 screens from the real game with a throwaway script (deleted after): desktop 1280 × 720, desktop 1920 × 1080,
  phone 2400 × 1080 at 405 dpi, phone upright 1080 × 2400. Pictures are in `export/audit_2026-11-11/` (gitignored).
- **Read** the rules and code for levels, classes, Skills, the night, sleep, fights, money, input, touch and menu size.
- **Ran** three test scripts: `unit_scene_beats` (5 of 6 pass), `unit_npc_sim` (13 of 14 pass), `unit_ui_scale` (11 of 11 pass).
  The full suite was not run.

Each finding says how it is known: **seen** (on a picture or in play), **code** (read in the source), **test** (a test run).

Not checked: sound and music, a real phone or tablet, play after day 23, the content of Books 2 – 10, speed on slow devices.

## 2. Bugs

**B1. The phone menu size of 250 % is never used.** (code, seen)
`Session.ui_factor()` (`game/ui/session.gd:79`) calls `ui_scale.factor(short, dpi, touch)`. It does not give the fourth
value, `mobile`. So `UiScale.MOBILE` (2.5) is dead code since commit fd178d2.
- A phone (short side under 5 inches) gets 200 %.
- A tablet (short side 5 inches or more) gets 100 %. The text is then very small (a 16 px font on a 300 dpi screen).
- `unit_ui_scale` passes because it tests `UiScale.factor` alone, not `Session`.
- Fix: pass `UiScale.is_mobile_os()` as the fourth value. Add a test on `Session.ui_factor()`.

**B2. The class offer title shows two brackets: `[[Class Offered]]`.** (seen)
`game/ui/system_messages.gd:169` gives the title "[Class Offered]". `game/ui/system_dialog.gd` adds brackets again.

**B3. The first log line shows two times at a new game: "Day 1, 06:00."** (seen)
`game/world/main.gd:119` adds it. `game/world/main_panels.gd:182` adds it again when the welcome page closes.

**B4. Two test scripts are red on `main`.** (test)
- `unit_scene_beats`: two fights have 4 and 5 ally shouts (rule 1 – 3). You decided the fix on 2026-11-01. It is not built.
- `unit_npc_sim` `test_talk_to_an_npc`: an NPC dictionary has one more key than the test expects.

**B5. Android: the Back button closes the app, and nothing is saved.** (code, not tested on a phone)
- No script handles `NOTIFICATION_WM_GO_BACK_REQUEST`. `application/config/quit_on_go_back` is not set, so Godot quits.
- The `back` action is the Esc key only.
- The game saves only each morning and on a desktop window close (`game/world/main.gd:526`). It does not save when the app
  goes to the background (`NOTIFICATION_APPLICATION_PAUSED`). If Android stops the app, the day is lost.

**B6. Text is hidden or cut.** (seen)
- A monster's name near the top is under the HUD bar ("Rock Crab 60/60").
- An NPC's speech bubble covers the NPC's name. It runs off the right edge of the screen.
- The player's head is cut off on the top row of a map.
- Upright phone: the HUD line is too long ("Hungry" is under "H: help").

**B7. A very short key press can be lost.** (seen, low risk)
Walking reads held keys once per frame. A press shorter than one frame does nothing. A normal finger press is long enough.

## 3. Game design and logic

### What works
- The core loop is sound: do work, sleep, get levels, class offers and news. The night page is the reward.
- The level rules are correct to the books and do what the design says. I found no error in XP, levels, class offers,
  class loss, consolidation or breakthroughs.
- Pace is fair: an inn worker has level 5 on night 7 – 8 and about 11 on day 22 (the balance sims). My rough estimate
  for day 194 is about level 25 (a calculation, not a run).
- The journal "Coming up" line tells the player where and when a story event is. This is a strong feature.
- The fight screen is clear: turn order, AP, move range, a plan before the blow.

### Problems

**D1. Sleep has no question, and it always goes to 06:00.** (code)
`MainScreen.sleep()` (`game/world/main.gd:445`) runs at once on the Z key. On a phone, "Sleep" is in the More grid.
- Sleep at 08:00 takes 22 hours. The player can miss the story event of that day.
- Z is next to X (drop), C (character) and S (walk).
- Fix: a page first: "Sleep until 06:00 (22 h)? Yes / No".

**D2. There is no way to pass time.** (code)
Space waits 6 seconds of game time for each step. One game hour is 600 steps, about 84 seconds of holding the key.
The game runs on hours: meals at 7 – 10, 12 – 14 and 18 – 22, story events in windows such as 15 – 18 h.
- Fix: "Rest 1 hour", "Rest until the next meal", "Rest until evening". The console has `wait <minutes>` already.

**D3. A new player has no goal.** (seen)
The start is 0 coins, no task, and a welcome page of rules. The first hint ("smoke from the old inn") comes only if the
player talks to the guard.
- Fix: a first task in the journal: "Find the inn east of the gate". Then "Find food", "Sleep under a roof".

**D4. No map, and exits have no names.** (seen)
An exit is a small arrow. A step on it costs 20 to 600 minutes. Only roads of 300 minutes or more ask first.
- Fix: a label at each exit ("Floodplains, 20 min"). A simple area map page.

**D5. Skills and classes have no text.** (code, seen)
- `skills.json` has 159 Skills and no description field. The character page shows only the name.
- 110 effects are stat changes, 71 are hidden XP bonuses, 13 are fight actions. No Skill gives a new action outside a fight.
- The offer page says only "[Cook Class Offered!]". A decline is for ever.
- Result: a level-up feels empty. The player cannot plan.
- Fix: one line per Skill and class (it can be made from the effects). Later: a few work Skills the player can see and use.

**D6. Fights pay nothing.** (code)
No coins and no loot come from a fight. The smith sells 3 weapons. There is no armour. Jobs pay 3 – 12 c.
Spellbooks cost 70 – 600 c. A fighter has no way to earn. Power comes only from levels.
- Fix: bounties at the Adventurers' Guild, parts to sell, one armour slot.

**D7. Defeat has no cost.** (code)
A knock-out ends the day. The player wakes at a safe place with 25 % HP. Levels still come that night.
This agrees with DESIGN ("no permadeath"). But a fight has no stake.
- Option: lose some coins or the held item, or wake "hurt" for one day.

**D8. Talk is easy to miss.** (seen)
The NPC's line goes to the small log at the bottom left. The card closes after each talk.
- Fix: a speech box with the face and the line. Keep the card open.

**D9. Every NPC card has "Persuade someone" and "Comfort someone".** (seen)
The text does not name the person. It does not say why or what comes of it.
- Fix: show them only when they can do something. Name the person and the result.

**D10. More classes make every class slow, and the game does not say so.** (code)
XP is split between classes that fit. The cost of a level follows the total level (hidden). "Accept" has the focus.
A player who accepts 3 classes in the first week is slow for the rest of the game.
- This is correct to the books. Fix: one sentence on the offer page.

**D11. The debug console is in the release build.** (seen)
The ` key opens it. It gives coins, Skills and spells and can kill NPCs. This is fine for an alpha.
For the Play and Microsoft stores, put it behind an option.

**D12. Sprites look the same.** See section 6.

## 4. Controls on Windows

- **W1. The mouse does nothing on the map outside a fight.** (code) `MainInput.world_tap`
  (`game/world/main_input.gd:138`) takes touch only. In a fight a click walks and attacks. Make a click walk and open a
  card everywhere.
- **W2. No gamepad.** (code) `project.godot` has no joypad input.
- **W3. No full screen switch, no key change, no display options.** (code) Options has sound, text size, touch and menu size.
- **W4. The menu size does not follow the window.** (code, seen) Stretch is set for the web only. "Auto" is 100 % without a
  touch screen. At 1920 × 1080 the HUD and the fight bar are small. Fix: Auto = window height / 648, in steps of 0.5.
- **W5. Cards need Enter or a click.** Let keys 1 – 9 pick a row.
- **W6. Only "H: help" shows on screen.** Show the key on each button the first days ("E: use").
- **W7. F (eat) and I (bag) open nearly the same thing.** One key is enough.

## 5. Controls on Android and touch

- **T1. Menu size: see B1.** Also add 250 % and 300 % to Options. Now the list ends at 200 %.
- **T2. Small buttons.** (seen, measured on the 405 dpi render at 200 %) "End turn" and the Skill buttons are about
  17 dp high. "Accept", "Decline" and "Continue" in the System dialog are about 18 dp high. The card and shop buttons are
  48 dp: good. Use `UiScale.touch_ui_px` in `combat_bar.gd`, `skill_bar.gd` and `system_dialog.gd` too.
- **T3. Back button and background save: see B5.**
- **T4. No safe area.** (code, not tested on a phone) The HUD is in the top-left corner and Wait is in the bottom-left
  corner. A camera hole or round corners can cut them. Read `DisplayServer.get_display_safe_area()`.
- **T5. Key hints show on a phone.** "H: help", "C or Esc to close", "PgUp / PgDn to scroll".
- **T6. Sleep is one tap in the More grid: see D1.**
- **T7. Upright (web on a phone).** The Wait button lies on the fight bar. Inside maps leave grey bands above and below.
  The Android app is landscape only, so this is the web build only.
- **T8. No vibration.** A short buzz on a hit and on a level-up costs little (`Input.vibrate_handheld`).
- **Good:** the floating stick, the context button with chips, tap to walk, the shop screen with the count strip,
  hold to attack.

## 6. Visuals and art

### What works
- One art family (LPC) for 163 character sheets, the ground and the objects. Nothing looks out of place in style.
- The main cast have their own looks. Monsters are clear (Goblin, Rock Crab, Skinner, moths).
- Night light and the fire glow in the market look good. The fight grid is easy to read.

### Problems
- **V1. The title screen is text on a dark colour.** No picture, no logo. It is the first thing a player and a store see.
- **V2. The inn inside is bare.** One plank texture, six tables of the same kind, no chairs, no bar, no kitchen things,
  no rugs. The inn is the heart of the game.
- **V3. The inn outside is a flat grey wall with a door** at the top edge of the map. No roof shows.
- **V4. Crowd people are copies.** Three or four Drakes with the same green sprite stand in one view. Give the crowd
  3 – 4 colours and clothes.
- **V5. Two market stalls use the same picture and the same sign.** Krshia and Lism should differ.
- **V6. The hearth in the market is a flat bright red box.** It does not fit the other art.
- **V7. Trees hide tiles and fighters.** Make a tree half see-through when the player or a foe is behind it.
- **V8. Name labels have weak contrast.** At night "The Wandering Inn" and "No Killing Goblins" can not be read.
  Give labels a dark outline or a plate.
- **V9. Menus are text only.** No icons for goods, Skills or classes. The character page is a list of lines. HP is a
  number, not a bar. Add an HP bar (and MP) to the HUD first.
- **V10. Space outside a small map is flat grey.** Use black or a dark pattern.
