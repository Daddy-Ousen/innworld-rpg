Privacy Policy

Innworld RPG · Last change: 2026-11-04

Innworld RPG is a free, non-commercial fan game. This policy covers every build: Android, Windows, Linux and the web build.

What the game collects

Nothing. The game does not collect, store on a server, send or share any personal data.

  • No account and no sign-in.
  • No ads, no analytics, no crash reports, no tracking.
  • No in-app purchases.
  • The Android app asks for no permissions. It has no internet permission, so it can not send data.

The sites that host the downloads and the web build (Google Play, Microsoft Store, GitHub, itch.io) have their own privacy policies. The game itself adds nothing to what they log.

What stays on your device

Your save files and your settings (sound, menu size, keys). They stay on your device only. The web build keeps them in your browser’s storage for the site. Uninstall the app (or clear the site data in the browser) to delete them.

Children

The game is not made for children under 13. It collects no data from anyone, children included.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new text goes here with a new date.
