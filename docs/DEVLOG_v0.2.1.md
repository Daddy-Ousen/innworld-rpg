Post on itch.io: Dashboard > Innworld RPG > Devlog > "Write devlog". Paste the text below.
Last devlog was v0.1.5. This one covers v0.1.6 to v0.2.1.

**Title:** v0.2.1-alpha: Books 8 and 9, big fights, quests and friends

---

v0.2.1-alpha is out. A lot has happened since the v0.1.5 post. Here is the short list.

**Story: Books 1 to 9 are playable**
Since v0.1.5 the story grew from Book 7 to Book 9. The game now has 1,614 story events, 335 characters, 130 places and 53 maps. The days after the Raskghar raids are in, from the victory parade to the battle at Liscor's east gate.
The story also tracks who is alive. If a person dies, their later scenes no longer happen.

**Big fights**
- The three doors at the Raskghar camp.
- Zel's last stand at the battle of Invrisil. Zel can live if you help him.
- The battle at Liscor's east gate, and the door thieves on the road.
- Bosses now have fear, bile and command attacks.

**Warnings before big days**
Big story days now warn you the day before. Talk in town, or check "Coming up" in the journal. Scenes show short lines while they play.

**Quests and friends**
- New Tasks page in the journal. Take tasks from Erin, Pawn and Pisces. Ceria teaches you a fire spell.
- 12 new spells. Some lessons need a friend to teach you.
- Erin's new dishes and drinks.
- Friends give perks: cheaper stalls, more XP, help in fights.

**Who you are, and a living world**
- You can pick a name, a look and an Earth background.
- NPCs have their own voices and talk options. Liscor Drakes are wary of a Human.
- Some patrons stay away from the inn if their race is boycotting it.

**Easier on a phone**
New bag screen with big cards, a shop screen with Buy and Sell tabs, tap-to-walk, and a context button for what is near you.

**New: Microsoft Store**
The game is now also on the Microsoft Store (free): https://apps.microsoft.com/detail/9NSJKRJRTP5J

**Downloads**
Windows, Linux and Android (apk). Android needs "install from unknown sources".
Get them from this page or from GitHub: https://github.com/Daddy-Ousen/innworld-rpg/releases/tag/v0.2.1-alpha
Old saves still load.

**What I need from you**
Please play and tell me where the story feels wrong or a fight is too hard. Comments are on. Tell me which book, which day and what happened.

**Next**
Book 10: a night fight against the undead at the inn, two new story threads off the map (Baleros and Chandrar), and a new map in Pallass.
