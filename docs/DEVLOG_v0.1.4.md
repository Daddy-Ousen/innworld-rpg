**Title:** v0.1.4-alpha: the story now bends when people die

---

v0.1.4-alpha is out. This one is about "what if". The books happen around you. Now they react when you change them.

**Canon bends (Books 2, 3 and 4)**
Key characters can now die, and the story keeps going without them. In Books 2, 3 and 4, events have a fallback. If a character is gone, another scene can take its place. Some scenes move to a new day. Some scenes do not happen at all.

Examples you can try: kill Erin, Ceria, Pisces or Lyonette in Book 4 and watch who is still at the inn when winter comes. Or cook at the inn stove on day 96 and see if the solstice dawn changes.

I tested this by killing seven key characters on one day in Book 4 and checking that the story still reaches the end.

**Class breakthroughs**
A hard moment can now push a class past a limit. Classes have trials and hints. Some canon scenes give a boon. Consolidating classes (for example Dragoon or Druid) now waits for level 10 in the source class.

**More classes and a bigger map**
- New classes: Hunter, Archer, Alchemist, Blacksmith, Teacher, Knight, Assassin, Rider, Gardener, Beast Tamer, Acolyte, Priest, Bishop and more.
- New road north from Celum to Invrisil, with a street, a Runners' Guild, the Crag Pig inn, a Merchants' Guild and Magnolia's estate.
- Long roads ask for a confirm, then play a short walk scene.
- The UI is bigger and see-through on phones.
- 35 new NPC looks and sheets for Books 6 and 7.
