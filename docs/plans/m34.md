# M34 — Phone controls, second pass (proposal, 2026-10-21)

Status: **accepted** (2026-10-21, ADR 0045). The user took the three recommended picks (section 5).
Presentation only: no core, schema or save change (hard rule 1). `Interact.options` stays the source of rows.

## 1. What is wrong now (from the code)

| # | Problem | Where |
|---|---------|-------|
| 1 | One flat list holds every nearby object AND every action: "Krshia (friend) — Buy salt (2 c)", "Krshia (friend) — Sell all 6 hides ...". A shop or a busy inn gives 15 – 40 rows. | `ui/interact_menu.gd` `open` |
| 2 | One tap on a row runs it at once (`TouchControls.activate_on_tap`). An `ItemList` has no finger-drag scroll, so a try to scroll picks a row. Attack has no confirm. | `ui/touch_controls.gd:353` |
| 3 | Rows are one text line high. On a phone that is under the 48 dp minimum (Google) / 44 pt (Apple). | `ui/interact_menu.tscn` |
| 4 | "Use" does not say what it will do. It works only next to the target, then shows the long list. | `COLUMN` in `touch_controls.gd` |
| 5 | Outside a fight, a tap on the map does nothing. | `world/main_input.gd:112` |
| 6 | A shop buys 1 or a fixed bulk (5); no way to pick a count. | `interact_menu.gd:46` |

## 2. What other phone RPGs do

- **Stardew Valley (Android):** default is *tap to move and tap to act*: tap a spot to walk there, tap a thing to use it.
  Other schemes add an invisible left-half stick plus an "action" button on the right. The player picks the scheme in
  Options. Its button hit areas are larger than the drawn button. The Android shop has a quantity slider.
- **Shattered Pixel Dungeon:** tap a tile to walk (path finding), tap an enemy to attack, tap an item to get a menu
  of its uses. Big tiles and buttons. Players complain that a stray tap can move them into danger.
- **Don't Starve: Pocket Edition:** tap a thing and the character walks to it and acts; a green "action" button does
  most non-fight acts. A later update added a stick as an option.
- **Context button (Doom RPG mobile, FF XV Pocket, many others):** one button whose label changes with what is in
  front of you: "Talk", "Open", "Take".
- **Touch size rules:** targets at least 48 × 48 dp with 8 dp gaps; main buttons in the bottom third, in thumb reach.

## 3. Proposal

### A. Tap the world (new main way to act)
- Outside a fight, a tap on a person or a thing walks the player next to it (`Pathfind`, the same step loop a fight
  walk uses), then opens that target's action card (B). A tap on empty floor walks there.
- A tap on the target you stand next to opens its card at once.
- The floating stick stays. Stick and tap work together (Stardew "tap to move + joystick").
- Every step is a normal move command, so time and determinism stay the same (rule 3).
- Each walk step can stop: a new tap, the stick, or Cancel stops the walk.

### B. Action card per target (replaces the flat list)
- Two steps: first the **target**, then the **action**. The card shows one target only.
- A bottom sheet (thumb zone): face, name, standing ("Erin — friend") on top; big buttons under it.
- Buttons in groups: **Talk** (voice options, Talk, Persuade, Comfort), **Serve**, **Trade** (one "Shop" button
  that opens C), **Learn**, **Other** (Appraise, Take, Sleep, Ride, Door trip). Each button shows the time ("10 min").
- **Attack** sits apart at the bottom, red, and needs a hold (0.6 s, a ring fills) on touch; with keys it asks
  "Attack Erin? Yes / No".
- A button fires on *release*, and only if the finger moved less than 12 px. A drag scrolls (ScrollContainer with
  touch drag). This kills mis-picks.
- Button height 64 px at phone scale (≥ 48 dp), gap 8 px.
- Keys and mouse still work: arrows move focus, Enter picks, Esc closes.

### C. Shop screen (own panel)
- Two tabs: **Buy** and **Sell**. A grid of item cards: name, price, how many you have.
- Tap a card: a strip at the bottom shows **− count +**, **Max**, the total price and one big **Buy** (or **Sell**)
  button. Hold + or − to count fast.
- The Sell tab has **Sell all** on each card.
- Uses the trade picks that exist now (`Interact.trade_action(prefix, good, count)`): no core change.

### D. Context button (replaces "Use")
- The right column's top button shows what the nearest target is: "Talk", "Shop", "Serve", "Sleep", "Take", "Door",
  with the target's name under it ("Talk · Erin"). Grey "—" when nothing is near.
- Tap: opens the card for that target (B).
- When 2+ targets are near, small chips (face or icon) show above the button; a tap on a chip picks that target.
- Bigger than the other buttons (96 px), bottom right, in thumb reach. Hit area 20 % larger than the drawing.

### E. Rest of the layout
- Right side, bottom up: Context (big), Bag, More. Menu goes to the top right (used seldom).
- Wait stays bottom left. The More grid keeps its 9 buttons, at 64 px height.
- Options gets **Controls: Tap + stick (default) / Stick only**. Stick only = today's walk, with B – D still on.
  Saved in the touch settings file (`ui/touch_settings.gd`), not in the save game.

## 5. Decisions (user, 2026-10-21: "Go with your picks")
1. **Scope:** B – D first (action card, shop screen, context button, with the layout of E). A (tap the world) and
   the Controls option come after, as M34.3. M34.4 checks all of it on a phone.
2. **Desktop:** the mouse and keyboard use the same action card and shop screen. The old flat list goes away, so
   there is one menu to keep. Keys: arrows move focus, Enter picks, Esc closes.
3. **Attack guard:** on touch, hold the Attack button (0.6 s, a ring fills). With keys or a mouse, Attack asks
   "Attack <name>? Yes / No".
