## What the text says (agents' summaries)
- **Wirclaw's village** (4.33, 4.34): no name. A Gnoll [Farmer] with cows, Fischer Cows (biting meat-eating cows)
  and goats. "A pair of farms, a few houses", a wall about ten feet high, thick barred gates, a watch post on the
  wall, a barn. Gnolls and Drakes live there. On a big hill near Liscor; Bird sees it from the inn's tower. No
  direction, no distance. Nobody in the village dies; the village stays whole.
- **Eater Goats** (4.32 G, 4.33, 4.34): High Passes monsters, herds. Scrawny, black-and-brown fur, slightly
  curved horns, bar pupils, bloody teeth, normal goat size. Very fast, dodge arrows, jump about 7 feet, stack to
  clear a wall, bite and hold on, eat anything (stone, metal, armour). Fearless, almost pain-immune. One goat is a
  match for a Carn Wolf and can kill an average Hob; a herd is a Gold-rank job.
- **The ossuary** (4.37 O): in the Ruins of Liscor (Skinner's crypt), NOT in the dungeon. An illusion doorway
  opposite Skinner's looted vault doors (Olesm sees it with Niers' ring). Behind it a small round room with a
  hexagon on the floor: stepping on it drops you down a long chute (the floor closes again). Below: a big hall of
  bone piles, a skull on each, swept and kept; old Raskghar bones. One exit: a long corridor with a trap that
  leads into the dungeon. A Raskghar skeleton with a broken-haft battleaxe lies at the exit (Calruz's axe).
- **The inn basement** (4.34): it exists before Book 6 (Ceria: the Horns sleep there). A trapdoor ("cellar door")
  in the common-room floor. The Redfang five sleep there from 4.34. No size, no contents.
- **Giant rats** (4.37 O): in Liscor's sewers. The user chose no sewer map (ADR 0028 answer 2): no rat enemy.

## Design
### Maps (all `confidence: guess` for layout)
| Map | Location | Reached from | Gate | Mood / ambience | Knock-out wakes at |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `wirclaw_village` | `wirclaw_village` (new, book6) | `inn_hill` west edge, 30 min | none | wilds / outdoor | `inn_interior` |
| `inn_basement` | `wandering_inn` | trapdoor in `inn_interior` | `wandering_inn.expansion_begun` | inn / indoor | `inn_interior` |
| `liscor_ruins_hall` | `liscor_dungeon` (the ruins) | `ruins_entrance` doors | `ruins.vault_found_looted` (2.02) | dark / cave | `liscor_gate` |
| `liscor_ruins_ossuary` | `liscor_dungeon` | the chute (one way) | the chute room is walled off until `liscor_ruins.hidden_chute_found` | dark / cave | `liscor_gate` |

- The illusion doorway is a map overlay: a `stone_cliff` tile over the doorway `unless_flags`
  `liscor_ruins.hidden_chute_found`. The chute exit itself has no flags, so it is one way (the map validator asks
  for a way back only from a gated exit). The 4.37 O event (M18.4) sets the flag.
- The ossuary's corridor exit leads to `liscor_crypt` (the real dungeon). `liscor_crypt` gets a gated exit back
  (same flag), so the validator rule holds and the ossuary is never a dead end.
- The corridor has one hidden trap (M13.T), as the text says.
- Objects use existing kinds: trapdoor = `ladder`, hexagon = `mat`, bone piles = `bones`, Skinner's vault =
  `stone_doors`, the Raskghar skeleton = `bones`, the village watch post = `plaque` with a sign.
- No spawns on the new maps. The goats come with the 4.34 stage (M18.3).

### Eater Goat (`eater_goat`)
- `leap` ability (M17.7): range 3, ap_q 4, cooldown 2. Fast and hard to hit (evasion high), fearless
  (`flee_below` 0), `pack`. HP and damage between a Carn-Wolf-like beast and a Hob (tuned against
  `redfang_hob`). Tags `beast`, `goat`. Voice: an existing sound.
- Creature art drawn in `build_creatures.py` (`"drawn": "goat"`): black-and-brown body, curved horns, four legs.
