## What Book 9 is about
The Goblin Lord's war comes to Liscor. Tyrion Veltras drives every Goblin tribe of the north south in front of his
army: Reiss the Goblin Lord (Az'kerash's apprentice), Tremborag's Mountain City tribe, Garen's Redfangs and Rags's
Flooded Waters tribe. His secret plan is to breach Liscor's walls with trebuchets, let the Goblins sack the city and
then take it for the Humans. In Liscor the Raskghar loot is shared out, the rains end, a Goblin Lord (Greydath)
wrecks the inn's tower and maims Bird, Erin goes to jail for a few days and the magic door is stolen and broken.
The Goblins who reach Liscor first camp around the inn under a "No killing Goblins" sign. The book ends with the
battle on the Floodplains: Reiss, Garen, Pyrite, Headscratcher, Shorthilt and Yellow Splatters die. Rags becomes
Great Chieftain of the mountain.
