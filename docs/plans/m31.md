# M31 — Signposts and scene beats: plan

Status: M31.0 built (2026-10-24, ADR 0044 "M31.0"); M31.1 – M31.3 to do. Source: `docs/AUDIT_PLAYER_2026-10-17.md` finding 2.
Needs the M30 speech bubble. One branch + PR per step.

## Goal
The player knows where to be before a big day, and a scene plays like a small moment, not one line.

## Today
- 72 stages: 25 fights, 47 scenes. A scene places NPCs and shows one `line` (`core/stage.gd` `run`).
- Nothing before a stage tells the player where and when. The news comes the morning after.
  Example: the Skinner stages (days 39 – 40, Liscor gate 18 – 23 h, inn hill 20 – 24 h) have no warning.
- `data/talk.json` topics can be gated by days and flags, but a topic shows only on a first talk and by chance.

## Steps

### M31.0 Engine [Opus · medium]
- **Foreshadow** (proposed event field, **ask first**):
  `"foreshadow": {"days_before": 1, "text": "..", "where": "liscor_gate", "hours": [18, 23]}`.
  While the event is pending and its `requires` hold, the morning page of that day (or N days before the window)
  shows a "Talk in town" line, and the journal gets a "Coming up" list with place and time of day. It never says
  the outcome. A cancelled event drops its line.
- **Beats** (proposed stage field, **ask first**): `"beats": [{"after_seconds": 30, "npc": "erin_solstice",
  "line": ".."}]` on any stage. `Stage.tick` sends a due beat to `combat.lines` and, with an `npc`, to that NPC's
  bubble. Scenes get a `stage_run` too, so beats run while the player stays in the area. Leaving stops them.
- Check: is `combat.stage_run` saved with all its keys? If a new key needs a save change, ask.
- Tests: `unit_stage` (+beats), `unit_director` or a new `unit_foreshadow`, `unit_journal`, `unit_system_messages`,
  `sim_canon_book1` (drift 0).

### M31.1 Signposts for every fight stage and the key scenes [Sonnet · medium]
- One foreshadow per fight stage (25) and for scenes a reader will want to see (the concert, the Frost Faerie
  banquet, the play in Celum, Zel's funeral, the Goblin party, the Silver Swords' cake fight).
- Lines say what the town could know: "The Silver-rank teams go into the ruins tomorrow. Some say the dead
  are restless." Own words.
- Tests: validator (a foreshadow `where` is a real map; hours fit the stage), `unit_foreshadow`.

### M31.2 Beats for Books 1 – 4 scenes [Sonnet · high]
- 3 – 6 beats per scene, short, own words, speakers from the placed NPCs. A reading agent gives summaries of each
  scene's chapter (no book text back).
- Tests: `unit_stage` data checks (every beat `npc` is placed by the stage), validator.

### M31.3 Beats for Books 5 – 8 scenes [Sonnet · high]
- Same as M31.2. Include the fight stages: 1 – 3 beats with allies' shouts (Relc at the gate, Erin on the hill,
  Lyonette at the three doors).

## Done when
Every fight stage has a signpost; every scene has beats; the touched tests and the validator pass;
`sim_canon_book1` – `7` (and `8` once it exists) keep drift 0; the user has played a signposted day and a scene.
