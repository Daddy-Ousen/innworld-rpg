## Check of the `6.` chapter ids (done in M37.P)
Read, not run: `tools/validate_data.py` `RE_CHAPTER` accepts `6.00`, `6.04D`, `6.12K`; the event-id check is
`^b{book}\.[a-z0-9_]+$` with the book number taken from the folder name (`book10` → 10); `depends_on` accepts `b10.`;
`f.stem` must equal the chapter id, so the files are `6.00.json`, `6.04D.json`. `core/canon_db.gd` reads every `*.json` in
`chapters/` and uses `order`, not the file name. A small Python test of the regexes passed. **No fix step is needed.**
M37.1 adds a load test for `book10` to confirm this with real data.

## What Book 10 is about
The weeks after the battle of Liscor, in spring. Five threads:
1. **Ryoka at Reizmelt** (6.00, 6.01): a northern Izril city beyond Invrisil. She runs letters, rescues the Pithfire Hounds
   from Wailer Frogs, and meets the vampire Fierre (an "opener", Serafierre val Lischelle-Drakle) and her sick family on a
   ruined estate. She gains no class or level.
2. **The inn after the war** (6.02, 6.03, 6.08, 6.09, 6.10, 6.11, 6.16): the inn is closed and barred. Erin's Skills keep
   Liscor folk out, and the Goblin dead rise each night. Numbtongue breaks the silence, walks through Liscor, and buries
   Pyrite's head. Erin tells her household she is from Earth; Lyonette says she is a Princess; the inn reopens. Numbtongue
   mines mana stone in the mountain and learns Pyrite's skills. Erin visits Pallass and finds Rufelt and Lasica. Ilvriss
   and Osthia plan against Az'kerash and Ilvriss leaves for Salazsar. The Gold-rank teams leave north.
3. **Baleros, Daly's thread** (6.04 D – 6.07 D): the Earth people in Talenqual: Daly's Bushrangers, Geneva's surgery,
   Luan's boat Runs, Paige's crossbows, new arrivals, and Niers learning the truth about Earth.
4. **Chandrar, the King's thread** (6.12 K – 6.15 K): Flos retakes Germina and Hellios without a battle, Nawal's sword
   contest in Reim, the banquet, the peace edict, and the rival nations gathering at Pomle.
5. Side scenes: Lyonette's [Worldly Princess] 17 (6.08), Apista and the Ashfire queen.
