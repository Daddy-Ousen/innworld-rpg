Status: APPROVED choices (2026-09-30), see "Answers". Build in progress. Hidden XP (duress, event windows) is a separate step: M17.8.

## Goal
Finish M17. Make the fights fair and long enough to play.
1. Fights last about 7 rounds, not 3: HP x2 (`rules.combat.tactical.hp_scale`, ADR 0027).
2. Enemies have moves in data (`abilities`), not only blows.
3. A level costs by the TOTAL level, with a hidden cap of 100 total levels (ADR 0022). The curve is retuned.
4. NPC allies get their own Agility (one default of 3 until now).
5. The balance probes run again on the new rules. The day-21 raid is checked again.

## Design (proposed; the open choices below may change it)
### 1. HP scale
- `hp_scale` 2.0 (a guess; the probes may move it to 1.5-2.5). One helper reads it: `Combat.scaled_hp(db, base)` = `roundi(base x scale)`, at least 1.
- Applied to: player max HP, monster HP (at spawn and in every ratio: flee below, escape below, HP bars), NPC HP.
  A ratio such as `flee_below` still uses the scaled maximum.
- Not applied to damage, armor, accuracy. That is the point: each side takes twice as many blows to fall.
- Flat HP numbers outside fights (bandage, cold, traps, fairy snow) also go x scale, so they keep their weight.
  (Open choice 4.)
- No save change. HP is worked out from data. An old save keeps its current HP; the maximum grows.

### 2. Enemy `abilities`
Optional list on an enemy: `"abilities": [{"kind": ..., ...}]`. All numbers are guesses (`confidence: "guess"`). Each ability names
its canon line in the enemy's `canon_ref` note. A small module `core/monster_abilities.gd` holds the rules, one `match` per kind.
- **`shooter`**: replaces the derived `is_shooter` (M17.6). Needs a `ranged` entry (`CombatDb` checks).
  Archer and shaman get it. Nothing changes in play.
- **`guard`** (a "shell"): NOT built (user: "No shell", 2026-09-30). The Rock Crab keeps its armor 4 and its scare rule; the Shield Spider gets the leap only.
- **`leap`**: `{"kind": "leap", "name": "Leap", "range": 4, "ap_q": 8, "cooldown": 3}`. When the target is not side by side, the leap is ready,
  the monster has the AP, and a free tile beside the target is within `range` (king distance) with no wall between: it jumps there
  (the move cap does not apply), then hits with the AP left. Uses `encounter.cool`. Shield Spider (roadmap), Ghoul (1.58 H: "leaps and claws").
- Monsters that fall to another move later (fear, poison) need a status system. That is not in this plan.

### 3. Level cost by total level
- `Levels.cost(p, class_id, rules)` = curve at the player's total level (min 1). `level_up`, `is_blocked` and the console use it.
  `Levels.xp_to_next(level, rules)` stays as the pure curve, so `unit_levels` keeps its numbers.
- A second class now costs more (class B level 1 -> 2 with class A at 5 costs the price of level 6). One class: no change.
  This is Ryoka's theory (2.41): the cost follows the total level.
- Hidden cap: `rules.levels.total_cap` 100. At the cap `level_up` stops (XP stays in the class) and no new class is offered.
  No message. The cap is never shown.
- Curve retune: see open choice 3. New optional keys `late_from` and `late_growth` in `rules.levels`.

### 4. NPC Agility
Optional `agility` in the `combat` block of `npc_behaviour.json`. `Encounter.agility` reads it, else `initiative.npc_default`.
`BehaviourDb` checks it (>= 1). About 24 numbers, all guesses. Fast: Klbkch, Gazi, Ryoka, Halrac. Slow: Zel Shivertail, Ylawes, Pisces.

### 5. Balance
After steps 1-4, run the probe table again and tune data only (enemy HP, damage, accuracy; the scale). Targets (to approve):
- 1 Goblin: level 5 wins on every seed; level 0 wins most.
- 2-3 Goblins: level 5 wins on at least 2 of 3 seeds (`sim_balance_fights` asserts it again); level 0 loses (canon: Erin runs).
- Razorbeak: every level wins.
- Rock Crab: stays a "do not fight it with fists" enemy (canon). It falls to a real weapon plus the shell rules, or is scared off.
- Day-21 raid: not winnable by one player (canon). With Erin, Klbkch and the waves it must last longer than the M17.2 three turns
  and still end as the canon event says. `sim_goblin_raid` must pass.
The fight log stays in `docs/adr/0027-...` "M17.7".

## Risks
- HP x2 doubles every fight. Sims with loop bounds (`MAX_TURNS`) or fixed seeds can fail. Mitigation: run the FightBot sims after step 6
  and fix seeds and bounds, not the rules (M17.6 did this for two sims).
- Canon stages were tuned for the old numbers (M14.8). The raid, the Esthelm siege and the Book 5 fights are the risk. Tune data first.
- Cost by total level slows a player with many classes. A sim that takes two classes may level later. `sim_balance_progress` and `sim_m6_done` show it.
- The user cannot see the fights in a cloud session. Ability lines and the guard flag are checked by tests only. The user plays them at home.
- The Book 1-5 text is not in the cloud. Ability canon lines come from the canon JSON notes; each is flagged for a text check.

## Answers (user, 2026-09-30)
1. **Abilities:** three fixed kinds with numbers in data (`shooter`, `guard`, `leap`) -- but **no shell**: only `shooter` and `leap` are built.
   Rock Crab: no ability. Shield Spider and Ghoul: `leap`. Archer and shaman: `shooter`. No save change (no v21).
2. **Level curve:** cost by TOTAL level, hidden cap 100, and a **gentler curve after total level 10** (`late_from` 10, `late_growth` ~1.12).
3. **Extras:** NPC own Agility (yes); flat HP numbers x `hp_scale` (yes); shooters retreat to cover (no); cornered foe fights (no).
4. **Hidden XP is M17.8, not here** (user, same session). Decided for M17.8: fight XP x0.5 at no HP lost, x1.0 at 10%, +1 point per 1% more,
   on the LOWEST HP reached, foe damage only, cap x2.0; event windows in data with tiers x1.5 / x2 / x3 (first: the Skinner nights, x2 for all
   actions); other classes (duress for cooks, runners, healers) come later. See ROADMAP M17.8.
   The XP supply changes the pace, so the level curve here is only a first setting; M17.8 re-checks it.
