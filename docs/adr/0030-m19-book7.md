## Answers (user, 2026-10-07)
1. **Rains: rain and flood.** An engine step adds a rains season: rain on screen and in the sound, and flood water you
   cannot walk on across the Floodplains maps. The inn hill stays dry. While the flood lasts, the door is the only
   way between the inn and Liscor.
2. **Door: several links and a Pallass map.** One door object gets several links; the player picks the stone. Links
   open and close by flags and hours. One small new map: the Pallass alley and street at the door end. This changes
   the portal JSON (rule 11): asked and approved here.
3. **The moths: a wave stage with an XP window, tier 3 (x3).** A fight stage on `inn_hill` on day 137; the giant moths
   and swarm groups come in waves; the Halfseekers, Silver Swords, Horns and the Redfang five fight beside the player.
4. **The dungeon dive: events only.** The dive, the loot (three artifacts), the Face Collector and the rescue are T2
   events with news. No stage, no Face Collector enemy.

## M19.0 as built (2026-10-07, local)
User answers: no rain duress (rain is look and sound only; the flood is the cost); the rain sound is made by a tool.
- **Door links.** `portal` is `{to, pos, power_flags}` (old) or `{links: [{id, to, pos, name?, power_flags?, when_flags?,
  unless_flags?, hours?: [from, to]}]}`. `Portal.links_of / open_links / link_open / link_powered / link_name`. A link is
  on offer while its flags hold and the hour is in `[from, to)` (wraps midnight). The interact menu shows one "To <far
  map>" line per open link; its id is `portal:<link id>` (`Interact.portal_action / is_portal_action / portal_link_of`;
  the old form keeps `portal`). `Commands.portal(gs, db, object, link_id = "")`: no link named = the first open link.
  Console: `portal <object> [link]`. A shut link answers `rules.portal.shut_line` (new, optional). Trips stay one shared
  count. `MapDb` checks ids (unique, no `:`), `to`, `pos`, flag lists, `hours`, `name`.
- **The rains.** `rules.rains {season_flag: "izril.rains", flood_flag: "izril.flood"}` (optional section, validated in
  `DataDb`). `Rains.on / flooded` read the flags. Rain: `Atmosphere.rain` (streaks, outdoors only, `set_time(at, winter,
  raining)`); `WorldView.setup(..., rain_flag)`. Sound: area beds may have a `rain` variant (`AudioDb.BED_VARIANTS`);
  `AmbiencePick.bed_for(..., rain)` picks it for outdoor and market places (caves and rooms keep their bed). Cue `amb_rain`
  (`ambience/amb_rain.ogg`) is synthesised by `tools/build_rain.py` (stdlib; ffmpeg converts it; `.import` `loop=true`).
- **The flood** needs no code: it is map overlays (tile `water`) with `when_flags: ["izril.flood"]` (M8.W form). M19.1 draws
  them; keep the rects off the door, the inn hill and the exits (the validator already rejects exits and objects).
- **No save change.** Both flags live in `gs.flags`. SAVE_VERSION stays 20.
- **Tests run:** `unit_portal_links` (new, 10), `unit_rains` (new, 10), `unit_portal`, `unit_map_db`, `unit_world_view`,
  `unit_ambience`, `unit_atmosphere`, `unit_interact`, `unit_sound_cues`, `unit_audio_data`, `unit_audio`, `unit_gated_exits`,
