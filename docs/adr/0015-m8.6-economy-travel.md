# ADR 0015 — M8.6 Economy and travel

Date: 2026-09-25 · Status: plan accepted (user, 2026-09-25).

## User choices
- **Sleep:** you cannot sleep outdoors, except on a campsite. Indoors or on a campsite without a bed: half heal. A bed: full heal. A collapse or a knock-out still happens anywhere.
- **Hunger:** one meal a day. Each hungry night lowers max HP by 10% (never below 50%) until you eat.
- **Road:** Celum gate → roadside camp 10 h, camp → Liscor gate 10 h. A paid wagon ride is the other way.
- From ADR 0014: coins, a goods bag, a paid room in Celum (Erin's bed and the camp bedroll are free), Erin feeds you on a day you work at her inn, earn from odd jobs and selling.

## Schema changes (approved with the plan, rule 11)
- New file `game/data/economy.json` (`EconomyDb`, `db.economy`): `goods` (id → name, confidence, `buy`, `sell` in copper, and at most one use: `food`, `heal`, `wear_flag`, `eat_now`), `shops` (id → name, `sells`, `buys`), `yields` (action → goods put in the bag), `jobs` (action → `pay: [min, max]`). A missing file gives an empty economy (toy dbs).
- Map objects: optional `shop` (a shop id; a shop that sells needs the action `buy_supplies`, one that buys needs `sell_goods`), `price` (only on a `sleep` object: a paid room), `ride` (`{to, pos, minutes, price}`).
- Maps: optional `camp: true` (a campsite: you may sleep outdoors there).
- `rules.economy` (optional; toy dbs have none): `copper_per_silver`, `trade_minutes`, `eat_minutes`, `haggle_share`, `rest {bed, floor, refused_line, poor_line}`, `hunger {step, floor, inn_location, inn_npc, inn_work_actions, hungry_line, fed_line}`.
- New action `deliver_parcel` (60 min, `running` + a little `commerce.trade`).
- Save v11: `economy {coins, bag, fed_day, hunger, haggled}`. Migration 10 → 11: no coins, an empty bag, not hungry, fed on the save's day.

## Design
- **Coins** are counted in copper and shown as silver and copper (`1s 4c`). `copper_per_silver` is 10, a guess: the Book 1 text sets no rate. All prices are guesses (`confidence: guess`). Both starts begin with no coins and fed on day one.
- **Trade** (`core/economy.gd`). The use menu lists a shop's trades ("Buy bread (3c)", "Sell herbs (3c)") instead of the bare buy and sell actions. A trade is `buy_supplies` / `sell_goods` for `trade_minutes` (5), with the good in the record's context; XP is scaled by the share of the action's minutes, like travel. The bare actions still work from the console and old tests (XP only). Haggling at a shop object gives 10% better prices there for the rest of the day (buy rounds down, at least 1; sell rounds up).
- **Shops.** Krshia's stall and the Celum market stall sell bread, firewood and winter clothes and buy blue fruit, herbs and firewood; Lism sells bread; Stitchworks (Octavia's shop, 2.08) sells a healing potion and buys herbs; the Rat's Tail sells a hot meal and rents a room (10c).
- **Goods.** Bread and blue fruit are food (eaten from the bag, key F). The healing potion heals 10 HP. Winter clothes are worn when bought (they set `player.warm_clothes`, the M8.W flag). A hot meal is eaten where it is bought.
- **Earning.** Foraging gives 2 blue fruit, gathering herbs 1 herbs, chopping wood 1 firewood (`yields`). The request board in Celum's Runners' Guild offers `deliver_parcel`, which pays 3–6c (seeded rng).
- **Hunger.** At night (`Night.run`, after XP): fed today, or an inn work action (cooking, serving, cleaning, chopping wood, carrying water) at `wandering_inn` while Erin lives → hunger 0 (Erin's meal gets a morning line). Else hunger + 1 and a morning line. Only a sleep into a new day counts: a nap within the same day adds no hunger (Erin still feeds you for the work before it). `Stats.max_hp` × max(0.5, 1 − 0.1 × hunger), rounded. Eating clears hunger at once. HUD: "Coins …" and "Hungry" on the status line.
- **Sleep places** (`core/rest.gd`). A bed next to you: full heal (a paid room is paid first; too poor = refused). Sleeping where you stand (Z) uses a free bed next to you. Else indoors or on a campsite: the floor, half heal. Else refused. A collapse is not refused. `Night.run` takes the rest kind; `Combat.night` scales the sleep heal by it. Debug and tests: the bed id `Rest.ANYWHERE` (`*`) sleeps anywhere as in a bed; older sims use it so they keep their meaning.
- **Road.** New map `road_camp` (location `high_passes`, `camp: true`): the road north–south, a campfire (warm; cook, keep watch), a bedroll (free bed), a dead tree, a stream crossing, an herb patch, loose stones. `celum_gate` south edge ↔ camp north edge and camp south edge ↔ a new road north out of `liscor_gate`, 600 minutes each. The walk is outdoors, so in winter it is cold (never below 1 HP). A knock-out on the camp wakes by the campfire.
- **Wagon.** `celum_wagon` and `liscor_wagon` by each gate's road: 40c, 480 minutes, to the other gate. The ride is a `travel` action (context `ride: true`); it is covered, so its minutes do not chill.

## Known limits
- No shop stock, no price changes over time, no NPC money.
- Erin's meal needs the work record at the inn, not Erin being there.
- The wagon runs at any hour, and does not care about canon events on the road.

## Tests
New `unit_economy`, `unit_rest`, `unit_travel`, `sim_celum_trip` (Celum start → deliveries → bread → the Rat's Tail → the road → the camp bedroll → Liscor → sell herbs; same seed, same trip). Old sims sleep with `Rest.ANYWHERE`; option-dictionary tests get the new keys.
