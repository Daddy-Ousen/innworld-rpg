## Decisions
- **Exits gated by flags (M13.0).** An exit can have `id`, `when_flags` and `unless_flags`. A hidden exit is
  plain floor for movement, pathfinding, monsters and NPCs. The flag cache is shared by every game on one
  db, so the validator reads raw exits. A gated exit needs a unique `id`, and its target map needs an ungated
  way back.
- **Traps are map data, not objects (M13.T).** Their state (`found`, `spent`, `disarmed`) is saved in
  `CombatState.traps`, changes only. All rolls go through the seeded RNG (rule 3). Save v14.
- **Enemy `look` (M13.0).** An enemy can borrow another enemy's art (`cave_goblin` → `goblin_grunt`).
- **The depths use existing tiles** (`cave_floor`, `rock`). A new tile would need new art.

## M13.T Traps (2026-09-28)
- **Engine.** New `core/traps.gd`. A map lists `traps` (not objects): `{id, at, name, damage [min, max], hidden,
  spot, disarm, once, rearm_minutes?, drop_to?, line?, when_flags?, unless_flags?}`. A step (not through an exit)
  onto an armed trap springs it: seeded damage through `Combat.damage_player` (it can knock you out), and the trap
  is found. A found, armed trap blocks the player like a wall (`Movement.step` gives `"trap"`); monsters and NPCs
  ignore traps. `once` traps are spent for good; a trap with `rearm_minutes` is spent that long; a trap with
  neither fires on every step. `drop_to` (a pit) moves the player to another map tile.
- **Change from the plan:** `rearm_minutes`, not `rearm_days`. The text gives minutes, not days (the ceiling
  spikes reset after about an hour, 4.19). No `kind` field: the view draws every found trap the same way.
- **Search and disarm.** On a map with traps the use menu offers the search (`Traps.SEARCH`,
  `search_for_traps`, 10 min, `vigilance.traps`): each hidden, armed trap within `search_radius` rolls
  `spot + spot_per_point × perception`. Each found, armed trap next to the player offers `disarm_trap`
  (15 min, `crafting.traps`): `disarm + disarm_per_point × dexterity`; a failure springs the trap (outcome
  `fail`). Disarmed stays disarmed.
- **Save v14.** `CombatState.traps`: `"<area>/<id>"` → `{found, spent, disarmed, sprung}`, only traps that
  changed. Traps keep their state when the player leaves the map. `_migrate_13_to_14` adds `{}`.
- **Rules.** Optional `rules.traps` (actions, search name, radius, per-point bonuses, lines). `MapDb` checks a
  trap: fields, unique id, a walkable tile that is no exit, no exit's arrive tile, no object, under no overlay;
  damage, 0..1 chances, `rearm_minutes` only when not `once`, `drop_to`, and `rules.traps` with known actions.
  `MapDb.flags_hold` is now public (traps and the view use it).
- **Depths data.** 9 traps: two runes in the rune hall (recharge after 30 min), two covered pits in the pit
  corridor (drop to the crypt worm tunnels at [3,16]), two ceiling-spike plates in the spike room (reset after
  60 min), two trapped books in the library (once), the acid grate (30 min). Each two-wide passage keeps one safe
  tile. Not placed: the fire room of 4.19. The acid "eats magic protection" (4.19), but there is no magic
  protection in the game. Damage and chances are design numbers.
- **View.** A found trap is a diamond on its tile: red while armed, grey when spent or disarmed.
- Tests: `unit_traps` (17), `sim_liscor_depths` +1 (the real rune, pit and search). The M13.0 walking test
  disarms the depths traps first.
