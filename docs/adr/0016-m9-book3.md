# ADR 0016 — M9 Book 3 (Flowers of Esthelm)

Date: 2026-09-26 · Status: plan accepted (user, 2026-09-26); M9.1 built, PR open.

## User choices (plan)
- M9 = Book 3 canon. Book 3 in four canon batches, one branch + PR each: M9.1 3.00 E – 3.05 L + 1.00 D / 1.01 D · M9.2 3.06 L – 3.14 · M9.3 3.15 – 3.20 T · M9.4 3.21 L – 3.25.
- Laken (Riverfarm) and Geneva (Baleros) are events only, like Tom on Rhir (M8.3): news and rumors, no map.
- Esthelm becomes a playable map (`esthelm_ruins`, from the road camp) with the siege as a wave stage (M9.3).
- Lyonette and Mrsha get `npc_behaviour` entries in M9.1; Zel later (M9.4). Data only, no schema change.
- Stage and hook choices are asked at the start of each batch. Any schema or engine change is asked first.

## Book 3 facts that shape the data
- Book 3 of the ebook: 28 chapters, 305,675 words, extracted to `canon/raw/book3` (gitignored). 1.00 D / 1.01 D sit between 3.05 and 3.06 in the spine.
- "E" in 3.00, 3.01, 3.11 and 3.12 is Laken Godart ([Emperor]), not Erin. His "Day N" counts from his own arrival, and the text gives no safe link to the game calendar (his snow and his avalanche point to different offsets). So Laken events use placeholder days from day 71 on, in order, like the Rhir thread (1.00C); each is `"confidence": "guess"` and its note gives his diary day. This also keeps Book 3 events out of the Book 1 and Book 2 test runs.
- 1.00 D / 1.01 D follow Geneva Scala ([Doctor]) in Baleros. Placeholder days (72–76), like 1.00C.
- Name clashes, kept apart: the maid Teresa (3.13) is not `teresa`; the dead Horn Marian (3.08) is not `marian`; Laken's Earth friend Teresa (3.00) is not an NPC. The Hob in Rags' tribe in 3.10 is unnamed and is not linked to `garen`.
- Erin stays in Celum for all of Book 3. In 3.25 she leaves on the wagon south with the Horns; she is home only in Book 4. The Albez door as a portal is Book 4 work.
- Ryoka never gains a level. The Florist's Horror ranks and level loss are `other` system records, not a new mechanic.
- Book 3 ends near day 88–90 (a guess). Winter lasts the whole book.
