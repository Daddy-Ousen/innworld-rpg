# ADR 0048 — M37 Book 10 (The Wind Runner)

Date: 2026-11-04 · Status: accepted (the user answered the four choices on 2026-11-04; merging the M37.P PR approves the plan).

## Context
Book 9 (M36) is done: the battle of Liscor is day 176, the Goblin Lord is dead, the inn is closed and barred, the magic door
is gone. Book 10 covers the weeks after (spring). Five threads: Ryoka at Reizmelt (6.00, 6.01); the inn, Liscor and Pallass
after the war (6.02, 6.03, 6.08 – 6.11, 6.16); Daly's Earth people on Baleros (6.04 D – 6.07 D); the King of Destruction on
Chandrar (6.12 K – 6.15 K). Day map, conflicts, regions and steps: `docs/plans/m37.md`. Source: the Book 10 ebook (rewrite),
read by nine agents; no book text in git (rule 10).

## Decisions
- **Day rule:** anchor is the battle, day 176. 6.02's "eighth day" is day 184; 6.09's "two weeks" is day 190. 6.08, 6.09
  and 6.10 are one day (190). All days are `guess` unless the text counts them.
- **Chapter ids:** the `6.` ids, the letters D and K, `b10.` event ids and `order` 100 – 116 need no tool or loader change
  (checked by reading `tools/validate_data.py` and `core/canon_db.gd`). M37.1 adds a load test.
- **No engine step:** door stones, the inn's closed state and the Drake boycott are flags (ADR 0030, 0043, 0047). M37.0 is
  skipped. A step that finds a gap stops and asks (rules 8 and 11).
- **Off-map threads, short version (user pick):** Baleros (D) and Chandrar (K), 8 chapters, 5 – 6 key events each, one batch
  per thread, off the map, no stage, no hook. Book 5 already has the Baleros thread (`1.00D` – `1.06D`) and Book 2 `2.22K`.
- **One fight stage (user pick):** the night undead at the inn (`inn_hill`, day 183). **Two hooks:** walk with Numbtongue
  (`liscor_market`, day 184) and the Gold-rank farewell (`inn_interior`, day 192).
- **One new map (user pick):** Tails and Scales, a Pallass bar (M37.2). All other new places are records.
- **Ryoka:** a Ryoka book. Her chapters add events and places. No class, level or Skill record: her [Runner's Wind] is a
  lie she tells, her "[Healer]-certified" is a certificate, [Barefoot Runner] never appears.
- **No named kill.** Book 10 has no new named death with a record. `sim_kill_book10` checks that the Book 9 dead stay dead
  and that early kills of Book 10 survivors cancel their events cleanly.
- **One entity, no merge unless the text says so:** Osthia Blackwing is the "Shieldscale" aide (text says so; a cover name
  note). Regrika Blackpaw may be Venitra (check the line). The new Quarass is the old one reborn (one record). Teres Atwood
  is the Book 2 record `teresa` (a name fix if the text says Teres). Lawes of Pallass is not `ylawes_byres`.
- **Adult content stays out of the data:** the explicit opening of 6.04 D and the contraception scenes of 6.06 D / 6.07 D get
  a neutral one-line summary or no event.
- **Event ids `b10.`; data in `game/data/canon/book10/`.**
