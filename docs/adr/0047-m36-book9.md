# ADR 0047 — M36 Book 9 (Tears of Liscor)

Date: 2026-11-01 · Status: accepted (the user answered the four choices on 2026-11-01; merging the M36.P PR approves the plan).

## Context
Book 8 (M28) is done: the Raskghar are broken, Calruz is in the Watch prison, the rains are on (day 163). Book 9 is
the Goblin Lord's war on Liscor: Tyrion Veltras drives the northern tribes south, the rains end, Greydath wrecks the
inn's tower, the magic door is stolen and broken, the Goblins camp at the inn, and the battle on the Floodplains kills
Reiss, Garen, Pyrite, Headscratcher, Shorthilt and Yellow Splatters. Day map, deaths, conflicts and steps:
`docs/plans/m36.md`. Source: the Book 9 ebook (rewrite), read by eight agents plus one fact-check agent; no book text
in git (rule 10).

## Decisions
- **Day rule (user pick):** Book 9's "Day N" counts Tyrion's march, not Laken's days. Game day = march Day
  + 163 (Day 1 = day 164). The ADR 0028 rule (+45) does not apply to Book 9. All Book 9 days are `guess`.
- **No engine step:** no schema change, no save change. The rains are flags (ADR 0030); the magic door links take
  `when_flags` / `unless_flags`; stages, hooks and beats exist (M28, M31). M36.0 is skipped. A step that finds a gap
  stops and asks (rules 8 and 11).
- **Rains end by events:** `izril.rains` clears on day 171 (5.53); `izril.flood` clears on day 174 (5.59).
- **Boycott ends by event:** `wandering_inn.drake_boycott` clears in the 5.49 batch (Drakes at the inn; guess).
- **Deaths:** kill effects for the named dead with records (13 names in the plan). **No kill** for Numbtongue (he lives
  in Book 10), Purple Smile (never confirmed), Osthia ([False Death]) or Calruz (no verdict in Book 9).
- **One entity, no merge:** Reiss is our `goblin_lord` record (a name note only). Greydath gets his own record unless
  the text says he is Book 4's `greybeard`; then the user decides. Lord Erill is not Princess `erille`.
- **Erin's [General] 6** is offered and cancelled in 5.62: a `system` record says so; she keeps no [General] class.
- **North thread** (Rags, Reiss, Tremborag, Garen, Tyrion, Laken) is events and news, off-map, as in Book 8.
- **Ryoka:** a City Runner at Reizmelt in Thereafter. No records, no levels.
- Event ids `b9.`; chapter `order` 78 – 99; data in `game/data/canon/book9/`.
- **Two fight stages** (user pick): the battle at Liscor's east gate (5.62, day 176, `liscor_gate`) and the door
  thieves at night (5.54, day 172). `xp_window` on each. Greydath's raid and the Rock Crab hunt stay plain events.
- **Three talk hooks** (user pick): the jail visit (days 172 – 173), Krshia's Gnoll gathering (day 173), judging the
  cooking contest (day 174).
- **Erin in jail on the map** (user pick): flag goals put Erin and the four Hobs in the Watch cells on days 171 – 173.
