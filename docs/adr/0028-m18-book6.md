- **The door does not change in M18.** The Pallass anchor leaves on day 121 and arrives after Book 6 ends.

- **Goblin guests cost the inn trade.** A `flag_bonus` of −1 for `wandering_inn.goblins_in_residence` in
  `rules.inn` (data only; negative bonuses already work in `Guests._arrive`).
- **The Hive stays off-map** (the Book 5 choice). Hive events are T2 news for the player.
- **XP windows (M17.8):** each batch may put an `xp_window` on its big night (asked per batch).
- **Raskghar:** `not_gnoll` keeps its id; its display name becomes "Raskghar" (4.36 O gives the name).

## Engine finding: event order inside a day
`CanonDb._build_order` sorts events by `depends_on`, then `window.earliest`, then **id**. To keep the book's order
inside one day, Book 5 put letters in front of its ids: 115 of its 140 event ids start `b5.z…`
(`b5.zzzzza_…`). Ids are hard to read, and a new event in the middle means renaming others. Using `depends_on` for
order is wrong too: a cancel then spreads to every later event. Book 6 has nine dense Liscor days, so this gets
worse. **Answer: a chapter order number** (M18.0, below).

## Answers (user, 2026-10-01)
1. **Event order: a chapter order number.** A Book 6+ chapter file gets one optional top-level key `"order"`
   (its place in the book, a whole number, unique in the book). Inside one day, events then follow the chapter
   order and their place in the file; ids stay readable (`b6.bugear_dies`). Books 1 – 5 keep their order.
   This is a JSON schema change (rule 11): asked and approved here.
2. **New maps: Wirclaw's village, the crypt ossuary, the inn basement.** No Liscor sewers (the text only
   mentions those jobs).
3. **The attack on Esthelm is a wave stage** at `esthelm_ruins` on the night of about day 117: Goblin Lord troops
   in waves, the player can help hold the town. The text tells it only in a report the next day, so the stage
   details are `"confidence": "guess"`.
4. **Laken's journal: squeeze Day 72 – 82 into days 118 – 119**, order kept. The estate battle comes before
   Magnolia sees the damage (4.38 B, day 119). Merged Book 5 data does not change (the Book 5 trick again).
